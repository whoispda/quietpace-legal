Quiet Pace — Support

Quiet Pace is a private running app: your runs, your data, on your device and in Apple Health. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Get help

Email cloud@clumsycloud.com and you'll hear back from the developer directly — usually within a day or two.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone, and — if you allow it — in Apple Health. Deleting the app deletes the app's own data; runs saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you remove them there.

Restore a purchase: open the membership screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Manage or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quiet Pace. Cancelling keeps membership until the end of the paid period.

Readiness shows "getting to know you": readiness needs a few nights of sleep and heart data from Apple Health (an Apple Watch or any device that writes to Health). It fills in on its own.
