Private by design

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

The short version: Quiet Pace has no servers of its own, and no account is required. Your runs and health insights stay on your iPhone, in Apple Health, and — if you leave sync on — in your private iCloud. Optional coach sharing is opt-in. We — the developer — never receive your data, and there are no ads, analytics, trackers, or Quiet Pace accounts.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Quiet Pace app for iPhone and Apple Watch (“Quiet Pace”, “the app”, “we”, “us”). By using Quiet Pace you agree to this policy.

What Quiet Pace accesses on your device

Quiet Pace asks permission for a few things, and uses each only to make the app work. Everything below happens on your device — none of it is transmitted to us.

  • Location. Used to record your route, distance, and pace while a run is active. Route data is stored on your device and is never sent to us. If you are a member, a coarse (kilometre-level) location is also sent to Apple’s WeatherKit to fetch local conditions, as described under Weather below.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit). With your permission, Quiet Pace reads workout, heart-rate, heart-rate-variability, resting-heart-rate, sleep, and running-form data to build your insights, and writes your recorded runs back to Apple Health. This read and write happens on your device; your Health data stays under your control in the Health app.
  • Motion & fitness. Used to measure your cadence during a run.
  • Weather. For members, Quiet Pace uses Apple’s WeatherKit to show local conditions (for the “should I run now?” read). To do that, it sends a coarse, kilometre-level location to Apple when you open the Today screen, at most once every 30 minutes, and only if you have already allowed location access. Apple provides the weather; it does not identify you to us.

What we collect

Nothing on our servers. Quiet Pace has no backend of its own and does not send your personal data to the developer. The developer has no ability to see your runs, health data, location, or how you use the app. You don’t need an account to use Quiet Pace.

iCloud sync (on by default, optional)

Quiet Pace can mirror your runs, saved routes, and training notes across your devices using Apple’s CloudKit in your private iCloud database. Sync is on by default so a new phone keeps your log; you can turn it off anytime in Settings. Data in private iCloud is under your Apple Account — we cannot read it. Turning sync off stops new uploads; existing copies in iCloud remain until you delete them via Apple’s iCloud settings or by deleting the app’s iCloud data.

Sharing with a coach (optional)

If you choose to share a program review with a coach, Quiet Pace uses CloudKit sharing (CKShare) to invite another person via a link they accept in their own iCloud. Sharing is never automatic. Only what you explicitly share is visible to the invitee; you can stop sharing from the app. We do not operate a coach network or intermediary server.

How your data is used

Your data is used on your device (and, when sync is on, kept in your private iCloud) to compute your training intelligence — readiness, training load, records, aerobic durability, and the rest. The calculations run on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Third parties

Quiet Pace does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not embed third-party trackers. It relies only on Apple’s own frameworks:

  • Apple HealthKit — your health data remains in Apple Health, under your control.
  • Apple CloudKit / iCloud — optional sync and optional coach shares, in your Apple Account.
  • Apple StoreKit — processes membership purchases. Apple handles payment; we receive no payment or personal information from it.
  • Apple Music / MusicKit — with your permission, authorizes and plays music through your Apple Music account. We do not receive your library or listening history.
  • Apple WeatherKit — provides local weather conditions.

Apple’s handling of any information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Quiet Pace membership is an in-app purchase managed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. Manage or cancel any subscription in your device’s Settings under your Apple Account.

Keeping and deleting your data

You control data on your device and in Apple Health. Delete an individual run inside the app, or delete the app, and that data is removed from Quiet Pace on the device. Health data is managed in the Apple Health app. If you used iCloud sync or coach share, manage or delete those copies through Apple’s iCloud settings. There is nothing on our servers to request, because we never held it.

Children

Quiet Pace is not directed to children under 13, and — as above — collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
