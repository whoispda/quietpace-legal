Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

The short version: Quiet Pace has no servers, and no account is required. Your runs, health data, and everything you do in the app stay on your iPhone and in your own Apple Health. We — the developer — never receive them, and there are no ads, analytics, or trackers. We can’t see your data, so we can’t sell or share it. You may optionally Sign in with Apple to save an account — that identity is stored only on your device; see below.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Quiet Pace app for iPhone and Apple Watch (“Quiet Pace”, “the app”, “we”, “us”). By using Quiet Pace you agree to this policy.

What Quiet Pace accesses on your device

Quiet Pace asks permission for a few things, and uses each only to make the app work. Everything below happens on your device — none of it is transmitted to us.

  • Location. Used to record your route, distance, and pace while a run is active. Location is used only during a run, is stored on your device, and is never sent to us or any third party.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit). With your permission, Quiet Pace reads workout, heart-rate, heart-rate-variability, resting-heart-rate, sleep, and running-form data to build your insights, and writes your recorded runs back to Apple Health. This read and write happens on your device; your Health data stays under your control in the Health app.
  • Motion & fitness. Used to measure your cadence during a run.
  • Weather. Quiet Pace uses Apple’s WeatherKit to show local conditions (for the “should I run now?” read). Apple provides the weather; it does not identify you to us.

What we collect

Nothing. Quiet Pace has no backend server and makes no network requests that send your personal data anywhere. The developer has no ability to see your runs, health data, location, or how you use the app. You don’t need an account to use Quiet Pace.

Signing in with Apple (optional)

You may choose to Sign in with Apple to save an account in the app. If you do:

  • Quiet Pace receives the private, Apple-provided identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your name. Both are stored only on this device, in the Keychain — they are never sent to a server (there isn’t one) and never shared.
  • You can hide your name; Sign in with Apple does not require it, and Quiet Pace does not request your email.
  • Signing in is entirely optional. Nothing in the app is gated by it — every feature that works signed out works signed in.
  • Sign out anytime in Settings, which deletes the saved identity from your device. You can also revoke access under Apple Account → Sign in with Apple in your device’s Settings.

How your data is used

Your data is used entirely on your device, by the app, to compute your training intelligence — readiness, training load, records, aerobic durability, and the rest. The calculations run on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it.

Third parties

Quiet Pace does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not embed third-party trackers. It relies only on Apple’s own frameworks:

  • Apple HealthKit — your health data remains in Apple Health, under your control.
  • Apple StoreKit — processes membership purchases. Apple handles payment; we receive no payment or personal information from it.
  • Apple WeatherKit — provides local weather conditions.

Apple’s handling of any information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Quiet Pace membership is an in-app purchase managed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. Manage or cancel any subscription in your device’s Settings under your Apple Account.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device and in Apple Health, you control it entirely. Delete an individual run inside the app, or delete the app, and that data is removed from Quiet Pace. Health data is managed in the Apple Health app. There is nothing on our side to request, export, or delete, because we never held it.

Children

Quiet Pace is not directed to children under 13, and — as above — collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
